Questions

The things people ask first

Do you serve Tulare and the surrounding area?

Yes. Tulare is home base and we cover the surrounding towns. If you are not sure whether you are in range, call (559) 679-7680 and ask. It takes ten seconds.

How much does it cost?

Every job is priced after we see it, because guessing over the phone is how people end up with a bill they did not expect. The estimate is free and there is no obligation.

How fast can you get out here?

Free on-site estimate, usually within 48 hours. Urgent work moves to the front of the line.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We will hand you the numbers before any work starts, and you should ask every contractor for them.

What happens after I ask for a quote?

Somebody calls you back, usually the same day. We set a time, look at the job, and you get a written price. No deposit to get a quote.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes. Workmanship is guaranteed and manufacturer warranties pass straight through to you.

Privacy policy

We collect only what you type into the quote form: your name, your phone number, the service you need and any notes you add. We use it to call you back about your job and nothing else. We do not sell it, rent it, or share it with any other company. If you want your information deleted, call (559) 679-7680 and ask, and it is gone the same day.

Terms of service

Quotes are free and carry no obligation. A quote is valid for 30 days unless it says otherwise on the document. Work begins only after you approve the written price. Workmanship is guaranteed; manufacturer warranties on materials pass through to you at the terms the manufacturer sets. Prices can change if the job turns out to be different from what was visible at the walk-through, and if that happens we stop and tell you before doing the extra work.
