What we build

Four capabilities. Built to connect, useful on their own.

A single project can be just one of these. A bigger engagement connects two or three so the parts share information instead of living in separate tools.

01 · Custom software & portals

Give your team and customers software built around the work they actually do.

A work queue, a selected record, its full history, and one clear action — shaped by how your business actually operates, not a generic template every trade uses.

  • Internal tools for your own staff: dispatch boards, job queues, approvals.
  • Customer-facing portals: requests, files, billing, status — for your customers, on your business.
  • Built to sit next to what you already run, not replace all of it at once.

03 · AI receptionists & agents

Answer enquiries, collect useful details, route requests, and support booking under your rules.

This is a distinct capability, not a feature bolted onto a missed-call textback tool. It's shaped around your business's own intake questions and escalation rules.

  • Handles routine questions and information-gathering, day or night.
  • Applies the escalation rules you set — urgency, service area, job size.
  • Hands off to a person for anything outside those rules. It never overrides a human on its own.

04 · Automations & integrations

Connect the tools you already use and make repeated handoffs happen consistently.

Most businesses don't need to replace their accounting software, their CRM, their phones or their calendar. They need those tools talking to each other reliably.

  • A trigger, a business rule, and a destination — visible, not a black box.
  • Exceptions route to a person instead of guessing.
  • Every run leaves a record, so you can see what happened and why.
