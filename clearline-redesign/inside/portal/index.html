What's in it

Signing up

A one-page agreement in plain language, signed on a phone, with an instant PDF copy.

Day one

A simple onboarding screen: your business, your site, your services, what's needed to start.

Requests

Describe a change in your own words, add a photo if it helps, and watch the status update.

Files

Photos, agreements and records, organized by date, in one place instead of a shared folder.

Monthly report

Uptime, completed work and inquiry counts, delivered automatically on the 1st.
