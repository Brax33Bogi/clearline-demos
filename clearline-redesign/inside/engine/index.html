Internal software

The engine

Built for Clearline's own outreach first: it finds businesses, checks their sites the way a customer would, and builds a corrected page — so a person only has to do the talking.

How it works, six steps

Find

Locates businesses through verified listings — real phone numbers, real reviews, not a scraped list.

Look

Checks the business's site on a phone, the way a customer actually would, and notes where it breaks down.

Judge

Filters out minor issues and keeps only the problems actually worth fixing.

Build

Builds a corrected page using the business's own real photos and reviews, checked against a 34-point standard.

Send

Sends a plain-text email, capped at 25 a day, business hours only, never to an existing client.

Listen

Surfaces real human replies and filters out autoresponders, so nothing gets missed and nothing gets double-counted.

What it isn't

This is a targeted outreach tool, not mass marketing. The daily send limit, the client exclusion and the reply-listening rules are enforced in the system itself — not settings someone could quietly turn up.

Worth noting

This is Clearline's own outreach system, not a product we sell as-is.
