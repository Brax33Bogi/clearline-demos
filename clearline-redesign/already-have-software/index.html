Already have software?

Already running Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, or something similar?

Good — that's usually a sign of a business worth building around, not a reason to start over.

What those tools do well

Jobber, Housecall Pro and ServiceTitan are real software, built by real teams, and for a lot of businesses they're the right answer. They handle scheduling, dispatch, quoting and invoicing at a level that would take real time and money to rebuild from scratch. Several of them, including Jobber and Housecall Pro, already document AI-assisted call answering as a feature — that's real, and if that specific feature fits your business as-is, it's worth using.

Where a custom build still helps

General-market platforms are built to fit a lot of businesses reasonably well. That's the whole model — and it's also the limit. They're priced per user, they hold your customer and job data in their system, and any feature they add has to work for every business on the platform, not specifically for the way yours runs.

What a platform like that is built for

  • A workflow common enough across your trade that it's worth them building once, for everyone.
  • Pricing that usually scales with your headcount, not with how much you actually use it.
  • Your data living in their system — exportable, but not something you control directly.
  • Support through their queue, for an audience much bigger than your business alone.

Where Clearline fits instead

  • The one workflow that doesn't fit any off-the-shelf tool, built around exactly how you run it.
  • A website, AI receptionist, or automation that connects to the platform you already use.
  • Software and data you own outright, built for your business specifically.
  • One person who knows your setup, not a ticket queue.

What we'd actually recommend

In a lot of cases, the right move isn't replacing what you have — it's connecting it to something that fills the specific gap: a website that actually converts, an AI receptionist tuned to how your business wants after-hours calls handled, or one custom screen for the workflow your platform never quite covered. We'll tell you plainly if what you have already covers it.
