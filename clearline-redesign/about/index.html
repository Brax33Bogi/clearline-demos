About

The person who scopes it is accountable for it.

Clearline is founder-led today. As projects get bigger, the team grows to match — deliberately, without losing the direct line to the person responsible for what's built.

BB

Braxton Bogenschutz

Braxton founded Clearline in Clovis, California, and personally scopes and works on projects across Fresno, Clovis and the surrounding Central Valley. He's the one on the intro call, the one who writes the scope, and the one you call if something's wrong.

Clearline is set up to build for established businesses with real operational complexity — not to fit one industry. The work has run through fire protection, industrial equipment distribution, commercial refrigeration and a range of other trades and practices along the way; none of those define the business, they're just where the work has been.

Today, Braxton scopes and builds most projects directly. As the work gets bigger — more moving parts, more ongoing relationships — that grows deliberately to match it, without turning into a call center between you and the person who understands what you actually need.

What that means in practice

Direct scoping

The person who writes your scope and price is the person who understands how your business runs, because they asked you directly.

Built for operational complexity

Clearline works with businesses that have real teams, real workflows and real edge cases — not simple one-person shops that need a template.

Growing on purpose

As Clearline takes on larger builds and longer client relationships, capacity grows with it — deliberately, and without pretending it hasn't happened.
